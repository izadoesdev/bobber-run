# Browser polish: findings and verification

This is a QA reference for maintainers, not a claim of complete accessibility conformance or a guarantee that the game has no remaining defects. It covers the TypeScript/Preact browser game. The pixel-art presentation is retained.

## Design evidence

The changes apply these recommendations to reproduced problems rather than treating a checklist as proof of usability:

- [WCAG 2.2: Keyboard](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/keyboard.html) and [Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html): native keyboard activation and visible focus for document controls.
- [WCAG 2.2: Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): relative-luminance tests with a 4.5:1 target for the tested normal-text colors, including the shared button hover effect.
- [WCAG 2.2: Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html): a rationale for letting players dismiss nonessential narrative updates. This does not establish that all game animation meets this criterion.
- [Game Accessibility Guidelines: replay narrative and instructions](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/allow-all-narrative-and-instructions-to-be-replayed/): conversations remain available in the journal after dismissal or completion.
- [Game Accessibility Guidelines: reminders of current objectives](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/indicate-allow-reminder-of-current-objectives-during-gameplay/): prioritize the current quest, with explicit optional-story goals and progress.
- [Game Accessibility Guidelines: consistent input methods](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/ensure-that-all-areas-of-the-user-interface-can-be-accessed-using-the-same-input-method-as-the-gameplay/): support keyboard and gamepad document actions, and use touch-appropriate instructions.

The 44 CSS-pixel story-action target and 12 CSS-pixel minimum story-action text size are explicit project regression targets, not assertions of universal legibility or complete WCAG compliance.

## Harbour visual pass

- Default typography now pairs locally hosted Nunito Sans body text with Fraunces logbook headings. The Jersey title stays pixel-styled. Font licences ship in `public/fonts/`; there are no runtime font-CDN requests. Settings offer Harbour, Pixel, and Plain, and retain saved Pixel preferences.
- Wood frames, parchment, focus states, buttons, and spacing use a quieter treatment without changing the pixel-art sea. Existing parchment and button contrast tokens remain covered by the luminance tests.
- Boat portraits frame visible pixels rather than transparent sprite padding, use a restrained three-quarter sway, and stop moving with Reduce flashing enabled. Cropped shipyard thumbnails and hull-role captions make the fleet easier to recognise.
- Skiff sails gain seams and rigging; the launch gains a raked cabin, fenders, a square transom, and mounted outboards; the trawler gains a supported gantry, net drum, and a clear working deck. Paint and plating are restricted below each hull's contact-edge height, preserving upper cabins, people, and rigging. Upgraded twin outboards replace the launch's original engine instead of leaving three engines.
- Detail text scrolls separately from purchase controls. Portrait-phone shipyards let the full detail card flow through the main scroll area rather than collapsing the description below a tall preview.
- `tests/boatModels.test.ts` covers all 18 hulls, base-model immutability, superstructure colours, outboard replacement, and gantry supports. `tests/sprite.test.ts` covers transparent bounds, centring, and large-sprite downscaling. `e2e/visual-polish.spec.ts` covers actual font switching/persistence, saved Pixel preferences, every boat portrait, thumbnails, and landscape/portrait layouts.

## Sea-life, encounters, and controls

- Whales (including calves), dolphins, jellyfish, and the four roaming giant silhouettes now use one stock attached to the actual rendered individual. Giant rays and squid retain their existing species; Moon Jellyfish, Bottlenose Dolphin, Humpback Whale, Sleeper Shark, and Ribbon Eel are appended to the catalog so old saved indexes stay valid. Server names and weight limits match. Normal rod, deck, hold, speed, and casting-range rules still apply. The ambient actor stops while hooked, does not render a duplicate during the fight, and disappears after landing. Ambient IDs are not saved as persistent escapees.
- Lighthouses offer daily glow lures/repairs, suitable fishing waypoints, and waiting until morning/nightfall. Villages offer bait/repairs and local stories. All six historic sites offer a one-time museum/survey reward and journal access without accepting a story. Driftwood, floating weed/lilies, vents, bones, sea stacks, basalt, floes, and icebergs offer corresponding supplies, samples, repairs, or survey rewards. Shallow habitats and glowing plankton supply daily bait/lures. This is not a claim that every individual decorative tree, rock, or wave has a separate action.
- Encounter actions revalidate the matching open dialog, live world object, proximity, speed, fishing state, valid action, and entitlement. Claimed finds persist in `visits`; collected removable props disappear. Daily services use the game day. Dialogs retain keyboard focus, gamepad document navigation, touch targets, and an explicit return to sea.
- Tab opens ship/hold. Q cycles nearby creatures; R3 and the touch **Next fish** button do the same. Clicking an in-range creature selects it instead of steering through it. The fish selection ring and floating target-name overlay have been removed; targeting and interaction/fishing prompts still work.
- School clusters use wider, validated separation. Initial swimmers use spread-out placement without a fallback pile when the shore blocks a position. A local spatial grid provides per-fish body clearance without pushing fish onto land. Observed or hooked hotspot populations are not replaced by a clock jump; reused generations reset displayed fish counts, and empty schools no longer leave phantom catchable-looking stragglers. This reduces overlap/pop-in; it does not establish zero visual overlap for every animated fin or tail.
- The server-admin-gated **Captain’s sandbox** adds a humpback call, dolphin parade, catchable jellyfish lantern party, giant-squid summon, 42-coin treasure, golden salute, and a `dontpanic` easter egg. Population caps limit repeated summons. Sea toys and progression cheats mark/persist the save as cheated, excluding it from records and the coastwide fund. Weather/time remain separate. Panel navigation/close controls stay above its scrolling content. `fullhold` excludes the Old One by ID rather than assuming it is the last catalog item.
- Schools now take eight simulation seconds to dive and six to rise. Runtime submergence drives actual depth, nose-down/up pitch, and receding size, including the Old One. Surface effects/glows stop as fish descend. Scares and time/weather changes use the same transition instead of a separate 1.6-second disappearance; distant unlit fish remain dim rather than vanishing at the edge of the boat's light. Deep returning schools are not cast targets until near the surface. Hooked schools stay up, and a natural dive retains the school's stock and swimmers.
- At the user's request, the Databuddy and Context boats were removed from the catalog, models, equipment mounts, portraits, fleet rates, and types, along with their unused logo module. The existing boat.dev sloop is unchanged. Saves selecting a removed boat fall back to the rowboat without resetting coins, catches, logbook, or other valid ownership. Catalog-derived validation drops obsolete fleet/hull/upgrade keys, preventing invalid fleet-rate totals.

Regression coverage: `tests/fishPopulation.test.ts`, `tests/shoal.test.ts`, `tests/diving.test.ts`, `tests/saveSchema.test.ts`, the catalog-wide model/server-limit tests, `e2e/interactions.spec.ts`, `e2e/diving.spec.ts`, and `e2e/sea-toys.spec.ts`. Controller coverage uses a simulated gamepad, not physical hardware.

## Returning-voyage clarity pass

This pass improves existing Continue → Journal → chart navigation. It is not a week-away player study, a new tutorial, or a change to progression.

- The title's existing tagline shows the saved game day, boat, and number of logged species. Reading it does not load, migrate, save, or accrue offline income. Preview validation reuses the save schema's boat, stats, and log fields; the clock has a finite, nonnegative preview fallback. Missing/unreadable metadata retains the generic tagline. The decoded summary is cached until the stored string changes, including changes while the title is already open.
- The journal foregrounds the existing shared next step before conversation history and the character portrait. **Show on chart** appears only when the guide has a real location. It centres the chart without setting a waypoint, starting autopilot, spending money, accepting a story, or turning objective markers back on. Existing optional stories and their separate sailing actions remain unchanged.
- Chart opening/closing shares one transient return context. A fish chart returns to its selected logbook entry; a journal chart returns to the journal, including the journal's original pause context. An ordinary chart returns to sailing. New voyages and Continue reset the old chart pan, zoom, filter, and return context; learnt fish waters remain saved normally. Catch-location caching includes the actual coordinates rather than only their count, so replacing a voyage's spots cannot reuse the previous voyage's destination.
- The chart has a contextual native Back/Close button, supports Tab and Enter, and retains Escape/M/controller close paths. The duplicate touch Close control is removed; touch Zoom remains. The footer wraps around its reserved touch space without splitting the Back label across several lines.
- The title scrolls safely on short screens and bounds its logo, summary, and menu at enlarged sizes. Journal decoration no longer pushes the next action below the initial enlarged desktop/landscape-phone detail viewport. Tests inspect actual text rectangles, reading height, viewport bounds, and action hit targets rather than just DOM presence.
- `tests/savePreview.test.ts` adds five pure metadata checks. `e2e/returning-voyage.spec.ts` adds twenty-two configured checks: readonly Continue preview and paused-story persistence, changed/unreadable saves, fresh chart state and catch-location caching, logbook and paused-journal return, unchanged coins/waypoint/clock/save while reading, hidden-marker preferences, honest missing destinations, native keyboard activation, simulated controller navigation, and enlarged desktop/landscape/portrait layouts.

No prices, rewards, catch requirements, boats, currencies, mandatory purchases, permanent HUD widgets, or new saved progression flags were added or changed. `src/econ.ts`, `src/game/rules.ts`, and 176 protected Godot files match this pass's pre-existing dirty-tree baseline.
